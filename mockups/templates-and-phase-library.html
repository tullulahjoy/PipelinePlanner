Overlaps to flag across the pipeline

Identify if any phase(s) in other procurements could clash problematically with this one, including another occurrence of the same phase. Select a 'Warning' for undesirable but manageable overlap, or a 'Critical clash' for unacceptable overlap.

Where a rule affects two phases, the rule will appear automatically under both phases. Editing or removing it updates both instances and refreshes warnings and critical clashes across the pipeline.
